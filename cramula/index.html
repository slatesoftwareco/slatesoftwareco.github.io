Cramula Support

Cramula makes flashcards, practice tests and trial exams from your own files or a subject you search.

In the app, open Settings and choose Help for a step-by-step guide.

Can I move my study sets to another device?

Yes. Use Settings > Save a backup, then Restore a backup on the other device.

Why is a card or question worded oddly?

Cards and questions come word for word from your file. Clearer source text gives better results.

Does it use AI?

No. It works by plain rules on your device, with no account and no key.
