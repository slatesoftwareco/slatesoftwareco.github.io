Cramula Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

Cramula makes flashcards, practice tests and trial exams from your own files or a subject you search. It is made by Shaun Krause.

What we collect

Nothing. Cramula has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and the developer receives none of your data.

What stays on your device

Files you add and the study sets made from them stay on your device. They are removed when you delete them or delete the app. Your device's own backup may include them.

Internet use

When you search a subject, your search words go only to the sources you tick: Wikipedia, Scholarpedia and OpenAlex (a free catalogue of research papers). The articles or papers you choose are then fetched from them, and free-to-read papers from the site that hosts them. Each service's own privacy policy applies to those requests. Opening a link takes you to your web browser. Everything else works without a connection.

Camera and microphone

Scan notes uses the camera to take pictures of your pages, and Apple's text recognition reads them on the device. Record a lecture uses the microphone, and Apple's speech recognition turns it into text on the device. The first time, your device may download Apple's speech model for your language. Pictures, recordings and text are not sent to the developer or to any online service; the pictures are kept with your study set on your device, and the recording itself is not kept.

Apple Intelligence

On devices with Apple Intelligence turned on, highlights and short versions are made by Apple's model on the device itself. Your material isn't sent to the developer or to any online AI service. On other devices, Cramula makes them by its own rules.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

Any change to this policy will appear on this page with a new date. Email slatesoftwareco@gmail.com with questions.
