SlateView Support

SlateView opens layout and DWF drawings and lets you mark them up.

In the app, open Settings and choose Help for a step-by-step guide.

Which files does it open?

DWF files. DWFx is not supported yet.

Do markups change my original file?

No. Markups are kept with the app's own copy, and you can include them when you share a PDF.
