SlateEdit Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

SlateEdit edits, marks up and signs PDF files. It is made by Shaun Krause.

What we collect

Nothing. SlateEdit has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and the developer receives none of your data.

What stays on your device

PDFs you open, your recent files list and your saved signatures stay on your device. They are removed when you delete them or delete the app. Your device's own backup may include them.

Internet use

The app does not use the internet for its own features. Tapping a web link in a PDF opens your web browser.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and questions

Any change to this policy will appear on this page with a new date. Email slatesoftwareco@gmail.com with questions.
