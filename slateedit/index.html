SlateEdit Support

SlateEdit edits, marks up and signs PDF files.

In the app, open Settings and choose Help for a step-by-step guide.

Is my signature a certified digital signature?

No. It is a visual signature placed on the page.

Where do my saved signatures go?

They stay on the device that made them.

Need more help?

Email slatesoftwareco@gmail.com and say which device you use.
